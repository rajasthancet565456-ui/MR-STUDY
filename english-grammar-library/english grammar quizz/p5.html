1. Simple Present

Active: Subject + V1/V5 + Object
Passive: Object + is/am/are + V3

Example: They sell cars → Cars are sold by them.

2. Simple Past

Active: Subject + V2 + Object
Passive: Object + was/were + V3

3. Present Continuous

am/is/are + V-ing → am/is/are + being + V3

They are building a bridge → A bridge is being built.

4. Past Continuous

was/were + V-ing → was/were + being + V3

5. Present Perfect

has/have + V3 → has/have + been + V3

6. Past Perfect

had + V3 → had + been + V3

7. Future

will + V1 → will + be + V3

8. Future Perfect

will have + V3 → will have been + V3

9. Modal Verbs

can/could/may/might/must/should + V1 → modal + be + V3

10. Modal Perfect

might have given → might have been given

11. Imperative

Open the door → Let the door be opened.

12. Negative Imperative

Do not touch it → Let it not be touched / You are forbidden to touch it.

13. WH Questions

Who painted it? → By whom was it painted?

14. Preposition Must Stay

laugh at → be laughed at
look after → be looked after
deal with → be dealt with

15. Causative HAVE

Have the car painted → Get someone to paint the car.

16. Double Object

She gave me a book → I was given a book by her.

17. Stative / Special Verbs

Some verbs such as smell, taste, resemble etc. require special attention. Do not mechanically force a normal passive pattern.

18. Singular / Plural Traps

binoculars, spectacles, tongs, alms etc. may appear plural in form and take plural verbs. Some names of games such as billiards behave differently according to usage.
