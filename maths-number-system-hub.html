CONCEPT GUIDE · EASY HINGLISH

Number System, made clear.

1. Number families: natural se real tak

Natural numbers (N) counting numbers hain: 1, 2, 3… (kuch books 0 ko bhi natural bolti hain, isliye question ki convention dekho). Whole numbers (W) mein 0 bhi aata hai: 0,1,2… Integers (Z) positive, negative aur zero: …−2,−1,0,1,2…

Rational (Q) numbers a/b ke form mein likhe ja sakte hain, jahan a,b integers aur b≠0. Isliye 5, −3, 0.25, 0.333… rational hain. Irrational numbers a/b form mein nahi aate; √2 aur π examples hain. Rational + irrational = real numbers.

N ⊂ W ⊂ Z ⊂ Q ⊂ R; irrational ⊂ R, but irrational ∩ Q = ∅

Example

−7 integer bhi hai, rational bhi (−7/1), aur real bhi. √7 real aur irrational hai, lekin rational nahi.

Quick trick

Set inclusion questions mein “smallest specific set” chuno: −4 integer; 3/8 rational; √5 irrational-real; 0 whole-integer-rational-real.

2. Face value, place value aur digit sum

Face value digit khud hota hai. Place value = digit × uski place. Number 6,52,418 mein 5 ka face value 5, par place value 50,000 hai.

Number = Σ (digit × place value)

Example: 8,04,306 = 8×1,00,000 + 4×1,00,000 + 3×100 + 6.

Digit sum divisibility by 3 aur 9 ke kaam aata hai. 7,422 ka sum 7+4+2+2=15; 15 divisible by 3 hai, isliye number bhi 3 se divisible hai.

Quick trick

Place-value difference mein pehle digit ki exact place likho, phir face value subtract karo. Digit-sum rule 3/9 ke liye; 4/8 ke liye last digits check karne hote hain.

3. Rational numbers aur decimals ka rule

Reduced fraction a/b ka decimal tabhi terminate karega jab denominator mein sirf 2 aur 5 prime factors hon. Jaise 7/40: 40=2³×5, isliye terminating. 5/12: 12=2²×3; factor 3 bacha, isliye recurring.

Reduced denominator = 2ᵃ × 5ᵇ ⇒ terminating; any other prime ⇒ recurring

Har terminating ya eventually repeating decimal rational hota hai. Non-terminating aur non-repeating decimal irrational hota hai.

Quick trick

Fraction ko pehle simplest form mein reduce karo. 6/15 ko 2/5 karo; denominator 5 hai, toh terminate. Reduce kiye bina denominator dekhna galat result de sakta hai.

4. Prime, composite aur co-prime

Prime ke exactly 2 positive factors hote hain: 1 aur number. 2 eklauta even prime hai. Composite ke 2 se zyada factors; 1 na prime hai na composite. Co-prime pair ka HCF 1 hota hai; dono numbers ka khud prime hona zaroori nahi (8 aur 9 co-prime hain).

Prime test ke liye √n tak ke prime divisors check karna enough hai. 97 ke liye 2,3,5,7 test kar lo; koi divide nahi karta, so 97 prime.

Quick trick

Ek universal claim ko chhote counterexample se test karo: “do primes ka sum hamesha odd” galat, kyunki 3+5=8. Par do odd primes ka sum hamesha even.

5. Prime factorisation ka use

Number ko prime factors ke product mein likho. 360=2³×3²×5. Prime factorisation unique hota hai (order alag ho sakta hai), isliye HCF, LCM, divisors, squares/cubes sab isi se nikalte hain.

360 = 36×10 = 2³×3²×5

Quick trick

2 se baar-baar divide karo, phir 3, 5, 7… se. Answer mein har prime ka exponent check karke multiplication se original number wapas verify kar lo.

6. Divisibility ke fast rules
  • 2: last digit even. 3/9: digit sum 3/9 se divisible.
  • 4/8/16: last 2/3/4 digits check. 5: last digit 0 ya 5. 10: last digit 0.
  • 6: dono 2 aur 3. 12: dono 3 aur 4. 15: dono 3 aur 5.
  • 11: alternating-position digit sums ka difference 0 ya 11 ka multiple.
  • 24: 3 aur 8. 72: 8 aur 9. 125: last 3 digits 000,125,250,375,500,625,750,875 mein se.

Example

432 divisible by 24? Last 3 digits 432, 8 se divisible; digit sum 9, 3 se divisible. Haan, divisible by 24.

Quick trick

Combined divisor ko co-prime easy factors mein todo. Sirf conditions ke labels mat yaad karo—check karo unka LCM target divisor tak pahunchta hai.

7. HCF aur LCM

Prime factorisation mein HCF common primes ke smaller powers se; LCM sab primes ke larger powers se banta hai.

For two positive integers: HCF × LCM = product of the numbers

Example: 12=2²×3, 18=2×3² ⇒ HCF=2×3=6; LCM=2²×3²=36.

Quick trick

“Sabse bada equal piece” ya “greatest measure” = HCF. “Kab phir saath honge?” ya “smallest common multiple” = LCM.

8. Number of factors, odd factors aur factor-sum

Agar N=pᵃqᵇrᶜ, toh total positive divisors (a+1)(b+1)(c+1). 72=2³×3², so divisors=4×3=12.

d(N) = Π(exponent + 1)

Odd divisors mein 2 ka exponent zero rakho. 360=2³×3²×5, isliye odd divisors=3×2=6. Divisor-sum ke liye har prime-power ka geometric sum multiply hota hai: σ(16)=1+2+4+8+16=31.

Quick trick

“Even divisors” count = total divisors − odd divisors. “Square divisors” mein har prime ka allowed exponent even: 0,2,4…

9. Perfect squares, cubes aur roots

Perfect square ki prime factorisation mein har exponent even; perfect cube mein har exponent 3 ka multiple hota hai. 540=2²×3³×5: square banane ke liye 3×5 multiply karo; cube ke liye 2×5²? Check exponents: 2² needs one 2; 3³ already complete; 5¹ needs two 5s, so ×50.

Square ka units digit 0,1,4,5,6,9 ho sakta hai—2,3,7,8 nahi. Square ke end ke zeroes even pairs mein hote hain.

Quick trick

Smallest multiplier: missing prime powers add karo. Smallest number to subtract for nearest square: neeche wala square; add karna ho toh agla square.

10. Unit digit ke cyclic patterns

Badi powers ka full multiplication mat karo. Units digit pattern repeat hota hai. Base 2: 2,4,8,6; base 3: 3,9,7,1; base 7: 7,9,3,1; base 4: 4,6; base 9: 9,1.

Cycle index = exponent mod cycle length; remainder 0 ⇒ cycle ka last term

Example: 7²⁰²⁶ ke liye 2026 mod 4=2; cycle ka second term 9.

Quick trick

Base ko uske last digit se replace karo, exponent ko cycle length se reduce karo. Sum/product mein har term ka last digit separately nikaalo.

11. Remainder aur modular arithmetic

Division algorithm: N=dq+r, jahan 0≤r<d. Remainder divisor se chhota aur negative nahi hota.

If a≡r (mod m), b≡s (mod m): a+b≡r+s; ab≡rs (reduce again modulo m)

Example: a mod 13=9 aur b mod 13=8 ⇒ a+b mod13=17 mod13=4. Powers mein repeating pattern dhundo, jaise 10≡1 (mod9), isliye 10⁵⁰ mod9=1.

Quick trick

Negative residue ko positive banao: −2 mod12 =10, kyunki −2+12=10.

12. Factorial aur trailing zeroes

n! mein trailing zero tab banta hai jab 2×5 pair mile. 2 bahut zyada hota hai, isliye 5 count karo.

Zeros in n! = ⌊n/5⌋ + ⌊n/25⌋ + ⌊n/125⌋ + …

100!: 20+4=24 zeroes. 25 ke multiples extra 5 dete hain; 125 ke multiples ek aur extra.

Highest p-power dividing n! ke liye wahi sum p, p², p³… se banao. Composite divisor (jaise 12=2²×3) ho toh har prime valuation ko required exponent se divide karke minimum lo.

Quick trick

Har step mein floor/whole-number division lo; fractions ko add nahi karna.

13. Repeating decimal ko fraction banana

Pure repeating: 0.272727… =27/99=3/11. One non-repeating digit ke baad repeat: x=0.12222…, 100x−10x=11 ⇒ 90x=11 ⇒ x=11/90.

Denominator: repeating digits jitne 9; non-repeating digits jitne 0

Mixed decimal 0.1(23) = (123−1)/990 =122/990=61/495. Decimal ko align karne ke liye multiply karke subtract karo.

Quick trick

Pure repeat ka block over 9s. Mixed repeat mein pehle repeat start tak shift; phir ek full repeat block aur shift; subtract.

14. Number formation, reverse digits & average

Two-digit ab =10a+b; three-digit abc=100a+10b+c. Reverse karne par 10b+a. Difference =9(a−b), jo reversed-digit questions ko short karta hai.

Consecutive numbers ka average = first aur last ka average. 101 se 199 ka average=(101+199)/2=150. Divisibility 3/9 ke liye digit sum; 11 ke liye alternating sum; 4/8 ke liye right-end digits.

Quick trick

Digit-puzzle mein letters ko digits mein convert karke equations likho; verbal conditions ko ek saath dimag mein mat rakho.

15. Common traps: answer se pehle ye check karo
  • 1 prime nahi hota; 2 even prime hai.
  • Co-prime ka matlab HCF 1, dono prime hona nahi.
  • Remainder hamesha divisor se chhota hota hai; negative aaye toh divisor add karo.
  • Terminating-decimal test se pehle fraction reduce karo.
  • Large powers mein exponent cycle-length se reduce karo; exponent 0 remainder ka matlab cycle ka last item.
  • Trailing-zero question mein sirf 5 count karo, lekin composite divisor ke liye har prime count karo.
  • Answer key ko blindly trust na karo—digit rule ya factorisation se final result verify karo.
